# Code of conduct

Repository participants must communicate professionally and in good faith.
Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
and publication of another person's private information are not acceptable.
Keep discussions technically relevant, evidence-based, and respectful of the
repository's research scope and licensing terms.

Security concerns must use the private process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
Other conduct concerns may be raised through the
[semiAIfoundry contact form](https://semiaifoundry.com/contact/). Semi AI
Foundry, LLC may moderate, close, or restrict participation in repository
spaces when necessary to protect contributors, users, or the integrity of the
scientific record.
