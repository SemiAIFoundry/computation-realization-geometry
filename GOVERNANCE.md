# Repository governance

Semi AI Foundry, LLC is the legal owner and licensor of project-created
materials in this repository. semiAIfoundry Research is an internal research
group within Semi AI Foundry, LLC and serves as the scientific steward; it is
not a separate legal entity or licensor. Fitih M. Cinnor is the identified
author and research lead for the released corpus.

The steward controls repository scope, citation metadata, retained evidence,
validation code, and release decisions. Outside code contributions are not
accepted. Public corrections may be considered under [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md),
but acceptance remains at the steward's discretion.

A citation correction must be synchronized across the README, CFF, DOI
crosswalk, JSON corpus, BibTeX corpus, and release metadata. Evidence changes
require provenance review, passing validation, and regenerated integrity
records. A release requires a clean tracked tree, green required checks,
reviewed legal and security metadata, an immutable version tag, and matching
release artifacts and checksums. Published tags and release assets are not
rewritten; corrections are issued as a new version.

Project-led checks, audits, and reproductions must be described as internal
validation. They must not be represented as independent review, external peer
review, certification, or a priority determination unless that status is
supported by separate, attributable evidence.
